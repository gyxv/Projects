ℹ️ How to Play

1. Click "New Puzzle" to create a crossword cube
2. Enter words in the popup panel
3. Click "Generate Cube" to create the puzzle
4. Navigate the 3D cube to find hidden words
5. Click letters to select and find words
6. Find all words to win!

Words can appear:
• Horizontally on faces
• Vertically on faces
• Diagonally on faces
• Through the cube in 3D
